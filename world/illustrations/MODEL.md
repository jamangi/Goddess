Composition/framing: panoramic 16:9 landscape viewed from a high threshold terrace, central labyrinth opening and nine rising districts readable at a glance, strong depth layers, city fills the frame, no empty text area required

Constraints: original fictional architecture; visually communicate ninefold organization without literal labels; classical Mediterranean silhouette but not a reconstruction of historical Rome; labyrinth must feel alive through subtle organic geometry rather than a monster face; no prominent characters; no readable text; no logos; no watermark
