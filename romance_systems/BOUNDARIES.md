# Roleplay Safety and Tone Boundaries

This file supports the human players; it is not an in-world legal document.

## Baseline

- All principal romantic and sexual characters are adults.
- Dark institutions, coercive context, jealousy, captivity, danger, and consensual power exchange are in scope.
- The roleplay distinguishes depiction from endorsement and institutional coercion from intimate consent.
- No character sheet overrides a player's live boundaries.

## Prose calibration

The approved mode is **high charge with selective explicitness**. Ritual, sensation, negotiation, reversal, and aftermath may receive close attention. Fully explicit depiction is reserved for encounters in which the details materially change trust, sovereignty, or power; otherwise implication and transition preserve momentum. This creative default never overrides either player's live boundaries.

## Before play

The players should privately choose:

- content that may appear directly;
- content that may exist only offscreen or by implication;
- content excluded entirely;
- the desired explicitness level;
- whether betrayal between the leads is playable or only threatened;
- how to pause out of character without it becoming part of the fiction.

## Recommended tools

Use a plain out-of-character pause phrase that cannot be mistaken for dialogue. Keep it separate from the in-world Open / Narrow / Closed language. Either player may revise a boundary between scenes. A quick debrief after a dark scene helps determine whether the fiction felt charged, tedious, or genuinely unpleasant.

## Narrative care

The romance should not rely on sexual violence to prove that the world is dark. Economic coercion, controlled movement, spectacle, divine entitlement, debt, compromised protection, and unequal law already create abundant pressure. Threats involving third parties should give those people motives and dignity rather than using them as disposable jealousy props.

The desired contradiction is: **the situation is twisted; the attention between the leads can still be exquisite.**
