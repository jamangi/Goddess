# Tension Engine

## A lightweight calculation

Use numbers to expose choices, not to automate characters.

For any active desire:

`pressure = priority × immediacy × uncertainty`

- **Priority:** desire score from 0–100.
- **Immediacy:** 0.2 distant, 0.5 approaching, 0.8 present, 1.0 now-or-never.
- **Uncertainty:** 0.3 nearly assured, 0.6 contested, 1.0 likely to be lost.

Two opposed pressures within 15% of each other produce hesitation, bargaining, displacement, or a ritual. A gap greater than 35% produces decisive action unless a secret or false belief changes the perceived values.

Example: Eve can free Vincent tonight, but believes he may leave.

- Be freely chosen: `100 × 1.0 × 1.0 = 100`
- Keep him close: `91 × 1.0 × 1.0 = 91`

The pressures are nearly equal. She does not simply decide. She drafts a conditional freedom, provokes reassurance, delays, or asks him a question disguised as a command. The scene ends only when new information changes one multiplier.

## Primary romantic tensions

| Tension | Eve's pole | Vincent's pole | Productive expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| ownership vs choice | access to him is legally guaranteed | affection must remain unowned | he makes a voluntary offering after a credible refusal |
| public command vs private surrender | must appear sovereign | wants entrusted authority | coded reversals outsiders misread |
| appetite vs ethics | novelty can outrun reflection | can leverage her curiosity | experiments include terms, end points, and honest debriefs |
| protection vs captivity | safety comes through her House | protection can become a prettier cage | he asks for protection of others, not more comfort for himself |
| exclusivity vs solidarity | wants singular importance | belongs to a collective struggle | she must help people who do not love her |
| manipulation vs seduction | wants him enough to concede | frames concessions erotically | he later discloses the strategic layer and accepts consequences |
| immortality vs equality | divinity sustains the hierarchy | equality may require divine diminishment | Eve risks becoming finite without demanding love as payment |

## The four relationship stocks

### Trust

Built by accurate disclosures, respected refusals, competent care, and costly protection. Depleted by disguised commands, surveillance, strategic omissions, and public use of private knowledge.

### Heat

Built by anticipation, novelty, asymmetry voluntarily reversed, risk, and precise attention. Depleted by repetition, entitlement, unresolved injury, and treating desire as proof of consent.

### Sovereignty

Vincent's stock of meaningful choices and Eve's capacity to set aside public role. Built through resources, privacy, exit options, vetoes, and separations that do not trigger punishment. Depleted by debt, locked doors, retaliation, and emergency exceptions.

### Mutual stake

The amount each has irreversibly invested in the other's future. Built by shared secrets, political sacrifice, co-created institutions, and rescue that changes the rescuer. It produces resilience but can also become mutual hostage-taking.

Do not collapse these into one "relationship health" score. High heat with low sovereignty is dangerous. High trust with low heat can become sanctuary without romance. High mutual stake with low trust becomes conspiracy.

## Feedback loops inside the romance

### R1 — Desired refusal

Vincent safely refuses → Eve trusts that yes means something → her desire intensifies → she grants him more room to refuse → refusals become safer.

This is the central healthy reinforcing loop.

### R2 — Indispensability trap

Eve protects Vincent → others cannot reach him → he depends more on Eve → she becomes more necessary → she interprets dependence as closeness → increases protection.

This loop feels tender while reproducing captivity.

### R3 — Strategic appetite

Vincent frames a political concession as an intimate experiment → Eve agrees → success increases his use of the technique → she eventually detects the pattern → every prior moment becomes ambiguous.

The inevitable audit of this loop should be a major rupture.

### B1 — Novelty and integration

Experimentation raises heat → repeated novelty creates emotional noise → debrief and ordinary care convert experience into trust → appetite stabilizes until the next meaningful variation.

Without the balancing phase, Eve becomes a collection of kinks rather than a person.

## Scene tension worksheet

For any substantial scene, answer:

1. What does each person want before entering?
2. Which desire is each pretending not to have?
3. What external pressure makes delay costly?
4. What can Eve command but wants Vincent to choose?
5. What can Vincent manipulate but wants Eve to understand?
6. Which relationship stock rises, and which is spent?
7. What consequence leaves the room with them?

## Anti-patterns

- **Frictionless forgiveness:** tenderness does not erase a structural injury.
- **Omnipotent Vincent:** system sight gives hypotheses and leverage, not certainty.
- **Cosmetic refusal:** a veto is meaningless if it brings punishment later.
- **Endless escalation:** novelty without integration makes scenes interchangeable.
- **Politics as lecture:** institutional information arrives as a demand, temptation, deadline, or betrayal.
- **Eve as kink inventory:** write the underlying curiosities and fears, not percentages.
