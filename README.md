# Goddess

**Goddess** is a dark mythic-romance roleplay setting about Eve, an immortal patron of the labyrinth games, and Vincent, the mortal champion she legally owns but cannot emotionally command.

The setting treats power as a system rather than a ladder. Eve possesses law, wealth, divine force, and public status. Vincent possesses scarce capabilities the divine order depends on: combat genius, popular legitimacy, an inherited sense for hidden systems, and the one affection Eve cannot compel without destroying its value. Their romance is contextually oppressive, privately tender, strategically dangerous, and never simple.

All principal romantic and sexual characters are adults.

## Design folders

- `characters/` — stylistic assertions for the two leads
- `world/` — mythology, political economy, factions, and system behavior
- `romance_systems/` — desire weights, romantic feedback loops, rituals, and consent under unequal power
- `story/` — campaign pressure and repeatable scene generation
- `decisions/` — choices reserved for the human creators
- `tasks/` — self-contained production tasks with recommended reasoning effort

## Creative north star

The world systems are pressure instruments. The primary product is the changing private culture between Eve and Vincent. Every political or mythic complication should either force a choice between their desires, change the balance of power between them, or make a private ritual mean something new.
