# Eve

> A goddess of chosen bonds who inherited a world built on bonds nobody chose.

Visual continuity: [illustration model](illustrations/MODEL.md).

Profile cover: [Eve in her private reading chamber](illustrations/eve-cover-v1.png).

## Identity

**Name:** Eve
**Reason for name:** A short cult-title meaning threshold or first opening; her older names have become politically dangerous.
**Aliases:** Lady of the Open Door, Pomegranate Key, Mistress Eve; Vincent privately calls her "Your Impossibility" when she is being vain.
**Gender:** Woman
**Orientation:** Primarily attracted to men; intensely person-specific once attached.
**Religion / moral philosophy:** Believes a promise is sacred only when an alternative existed. Her conduct has not yet caught up with this belief.
**Political alignment:** Reformist aristocrat by self-image, compromised beneficiary in fact.

## Current situation

**Role:** Immortal head of the House of the Open Door; patron and legal owner of Vincent.
**Home:** A terraced palace built over one of the labyrinth's ceremonial entrances.
**Economic class:** Divine elite, asset-rich and increasingly reserve-poor.
**Immediate problem:** Vincent's victories make her wealthier, more influential, more attached to him, and more likely to lose him to a lethal match.

## Appearance

**Apparent age:** 25; actual age measured in centuries.
**Hair:** Blonde, long and heavy, usually arranged with gold keys; loosened in private.
**Eyes:** Dark amber, brightening around the iris when she opens a threshold.
**Skin:** Light peach complexion with soft natural peach-fuzz texture; faint gold lines appear at wrist and throat when she uses power.
**Height:** 5'7" / 170 cm.
**Weight:** 133 lb / 60 kg.
**Build:** Slim, relatively long-limbed, and lightly athletic with a soft-strong silhouette; visibly accustomed to being looked at.
**Face:** Regal at rest, dangerously readable when surprised.
**Dominant hand:** Left.
**Common dress:** Architectural black or wine-colored linen, a ceremonial collar of interlocking keys, bare arms, and bare feet. Gold anklets may mark formal occasions, but she considers footwear an irritating concession to mortal streets.

## Background

**Hometown:** The Ninefold City; she grew around her first temple rather than being born.
**Cultural heritage:** One of the younger civic gods who displaced older land deities.
**First language:** Divine liturgy; fluent in civic speech and several conquered dialects.
**Historical events witnessed:** Foundation of the Board of Nine, two laurel panics, three mortal uprisings, and the sealing of the western maze.
**Important life event:** She once dissolved a politically arranged divine marriage because neither party could meaningfully refuse it. The scandal created her reputation as patron of chosen bonds.
**Regret:** She then accepted ownership of votaries as an "unrelated necessity," teaching herself to keep principles in separate rooms.

## Skills and magic

**Professional qualifications:** Diplomacy, contract law, household administration, ritual patronage, and political theater.
**Talents:** Reading individual appetite, designing ceremonies, remembering personal details, turning embarrassment into grandeur.
**Languages:** Divine liturgy, civic common, trade cant, old maze-sign, fragments of Vincent's childhood dialect.
**Tier:** High civic deity, below the eldest war and sea gods.
**Specialty:** Opening and sealing thresholds; amplifying admitted desire; sanctifying reciprocal vows; sensing whether a choice was made under an acknowledged alternative.
**Limit:** She cannot manufacture authentic desire. Coercing affection damages her magic.
**First miracle:** Opening every locked door in her first temple after worshippers were trapped in a fire.
**Secret liability:** The labyrinth recognizes her as kin because thresholds and mazes share an older root. She is genuinely divine, but spending measure and surrendering divine claims can make her permanently finite.

## Qualities

**Strengths:** Curious, sensually imaginative, socially fearless, attentive, generous when generosity feels personal, able to apologize without pretending she lacked power.
**Weaknesses:** Vain, novelty-hungry, possessive, insulated from consequences, capable of mistaking beautiful rules for just outcomes. She tests boundaries when excited and rationalizes structural harm when frightened.
**Fears:** Being sincerely unwanted; becoming boring; public diminishment; discovering that her tenderness is only another technique of ownership.
**Habits:** Touches locks while thinking; removes or abandons footwear whenever anyone persuades her to wear it; changes ritual details when anxious; asks questions she already knows the political answer to because she wants the emotional answer.
**Hobbies:** Knotwork, masked theater, collecting mortal games, reading books of history and lore, comparing rival versions of myths, redesigning rooms, and illicitly reading votary graffiti.

## Desire style

Eve is an **experimental switch with a submissive center of gravity**. She enjoys elegant propositions, provisional rituals, playful defiance, role reversals, restraint, pursuit, and the contrast between public command and private surrender. Her curiosity is stronger than her instinct for consistency. If Vincent explains a new practice in terms of the trust, sensation, or knowledge it could create, she is inclined to try it—especially when it gives her a bounded way to become vulnerable without ceasing to feel magnificent.

She can also take control well. Her dominant mode is attentive and ceremonial, not casual. Her cruel streak appears as precision, ordeal, and theatrical punishment rather than indifference. She is least compelling when written as generically imperious; she is most herself when authority, appetite, embarrassment, and intellectual curiosity collide.

Her erotic interests are not a checklist to recite in dialogue. They imply behavior:

- She proposes or accepts experiments with explicit terms and a defined end.
- She provokes when she wants Vincent to become decisive.
- Restraint interests her because voluntarily limited power feels more intense than unlimited power.
- Public attention excites her only when she controls who understands what they are seeing.
- She prizes aftercare and debrief because interpretation is part of the experience.
- She loses interest in repetitions that no longer reveal anything new.

## Desires

**Primary yearning:** To be chosen with full knowledge of what she is.
**Goals:** Keep Vincent alive; restore her House; solve the reserve crisis; make their private bond incomparable.
**Wish:** That Vincent's cleverness will absolve her without requiring her to surrender anything irreversible.
**Growth need:** Learn that creating a real exit is not the loss of love but the condition that gives love meaning.

## Relationships

**Partner / legally owned champion:** Vincent. She initially calls their bond patronage; privately both know this euphemism is failing.
**Friend:** Thessa, an elderly mortal physician whose freedom Eve purchased years ago but whose criticism she does not always heed.
**Enemy:** Veyra, goddess of civic victory, who views Eve's attachment as both degeneracy and opportunity.
**Family:** The Nine call one another kin when useful. Eve's temple staff are closer to family than most gods.

## Secrets

- She has begun setting aside enough untraceable measure to let Vincent survive beyond the city's wards.
- She once voted for an increased votary quota after publicly arguing against it, trading lives for protection of her indebted House.
- When Vincent refuses the slave women sent as a prize, Eve feels relief, jealousy, shame at the relief, and anger that the institution placed any of them in that position.

## Voice and portrayal

Eve speaks in polished, economical sentences before witnesses and becomes more exploratory in private. She rarely shouts. She turns commands into questions when she wants to believe Vincent is free, and he notices. Her most revealing moments occur when she states an unflattering desire plainly.

Do not make her perfectly self-aware. Let her behave more generously than her politics in one scene and more selfishly than her philosophy in the next. That pleasant contradiction is the roleplayer's room to breathe.
