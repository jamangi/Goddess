# Approved Canon Decisions

All initial creator approvals were confirmed on September 3, 2026. These decisions are now canon rather than provisional defaults.

## A1 — Ending shape

**Approved:** Dark hopeful. Eve surrenders part of her immortality, ownership ends, and the political order remains difficult enough for future play.

## A2 — Eve's level of culpability

**Approved:** Compromised reformer. She has personally voted for lethal quotas and benefited knowingly, but did not design the institution and has also saved lives within it.

## A3 — Historical distance

**Approved:** Fully invented mythic Mediterranean setting with a classical silhouette, not alternate-history Rome. This permits Minotaur logic, visible gods, and a fiscal system designed around the theme without inviting factual expectations.

## A4 — Sexual explicitness in the eventual prose

**Approved:** High charge, selective explicitness. Use ritual, sensation, negotiation, reversal, and aftermath in detail; reserve fully explicit depiction for scenes where it changes trust or power. Live player boundaries always supersede this prose calibration.

## A5 — Exclusivity

**Approved:** The leads evolve toward freely chosen monogamy. Third parties can create social pressure and jealousy but are never disposable sexual props.

## A6 — Vincent's lost family

**Approved:** His younger sister Mara survives among the Uncounted and distrusts both leads. She personalizes the political cost without becoming a simple hostage.

## A7 — Truth of the stolen heart

**Approved:** Orun willingly offered his heart to Ilyra, but she still had to kill him to complete the transfer. This preserves her transgressive act while making divine original-sin theology a deliberate distortion.

## A8 — Eve's true nature

**Approved:** Eve is genuinely divine but can become finite by spending measure and surrendering claims.

## Change control

Future open questions should be added only when different answers would materially change scenes, not merely add lore. If an approved decision changes, record the new decision and the story-bible files affected by it.
