# Creator Approvals

Nothing here blocks initial play. Recommended defaults are already reflected in the story bible and can be changed whenever the players discover a more exciting answer.

## A1 — Ending shape

**Recommended default:** Dark hopeful. Eve surrenders part of her immortality, ownership ends, and the political order remains difficult enough for future play.

Alternatives: tragic divine, corrupting romance, or revolutionary separation. See `story/OUTER_STORY.md`.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## A2 — Eve's level of culpability

**Recommended default:** Compromised reformer. She has personally voted for lethal quotas and benefited knowingly, but did not design the institution and has also saved lives within it.

Stronger darkness: make her a former architect of the present votary system.
Softer darkness: make her inherit the House only shortly before meeting Vincent.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## A3 — Historical distance

**Recommended default:** Fully invented mythic Mediterranean setting with a classical silhouette, not alternate-history Rome. This permits Minotaur logic, visible gods, and a fiscal system designed around the theme without inviting factual expectations.

Alternative: explicitly set it in a fantastical 70s BCE Mediterranean world and connect the climax to a recognizable servile revolt.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## A4 — Sexual explicitness in the eventual prose

**Recommended default:** High charge, selective explicitness. Use ritual, sensation, negotiation, reversal, and aftermath in detail; reserve fully explicit depiction for scenes where it changes trust or power.

Alternatives: fade-to-black romance or consistently explicit erotic roleplay.

This choice belongs to both players and should be revisited after the first session.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## A5 — Exclusivity

**Recommended default:** The leads evolve toward freely chosen monogamy. Third parties can create social pressure and jealousy but are never disposable sexual props.

Alternative: negotiated non-monogamy, which would require redesigning Eve's possessiveness as a recurring growth arena.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## A6 — Vincent's lost family

**Recommended default:** His younger sister Mara survives among the Uncounted and distrusts both leads. She personalizes the political cost without becoming a simple hostage.

Alternative: Mara died during annexation; someone in the Uncounted is using her name to recruit Vincent.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## A7 — Truth of the stolen heart

**Recommended default:** Orun willingly offered his heart to Ilyra, but she still had to kill him to complete the transfer. This preserves her transgressive act while making divine original-sin theology a deliberate distortion.

Alternatives: Ilyra killed him without consent for defensible reasons; or the truth remains permanently unknowable.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## A8 — Eve's true nature

**Recommended default:** She is genuinely divine but can become finite by spending measure and surrendering claims.

Alternative: all gods are former mortals whose divinity is maintained by a concealed fiscal technology. This makes the class critique sharper and the mythology less numinous.

**Creator decision:** Pending.

## Approval note format

Replace `Pending` with a decision and, if useful, one sentence of intent. New open questions should be added only when different answers would materially change scenes, not merely add lore.
