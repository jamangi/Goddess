# Method and measures

## Evidence discipline

Every substantive claim needs an ID, a source anchor, an interpretation, an alternative explanation, a confidence label, and something that would change the assessment.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observation | Directly visible textual feature; quote or reference it. |
| Self-report | Something the writer explicitly says about their intentions or experience. |
| Hypothesis | A proposed explanation of choices; never a fact about the person. |
| Unknown | Missing evidence, not a zero or a negative trait. |

Confidence is qualitative. **Low:** a plausible reading of one ambiguous instance. **Moderate:** clear local evidence or recurrence across independent posts, with alternatives considered. **High:** strong corroboration across contexts; reserve claims about stated intentions for direct self-report. No confidence label turns fiction into a psychological test.

Do not infer effort, drafting time, competence, or interest from word counts alone. Attention on the page is observable; time and motivation are not. Omission only becomes evidence when the omitted subject had a clear opportunity to appear. An unfinished opening cannot show whether its author can write reciprocal dialogue or aftermath.

## Measures

Use 0–3 only for local descriptive prominence: **0** absent despite opportunity; **1** brief/implicit; **2** developed/explicit; **3** organizing feature or recurrent emphasis. Use **N/A** when there was no opportunity, **unknown** when context is missing. These are editorial judgments, not calibrated psychological quantities. Do not add them into a single quality or compatibility score.

| Measure | What to record |
|---|---|
| Attention allocation | Word counts by stable source unit; one primary function per unit for totals, optional overlapping secondary tags. Name the counting rule. |
| Interior explanation | How much the prose explains motives versus lets readers infer them from action. |
| Embodied specificity | Concrete sensory or physical details, distinguishing functional detail from repeated description. |
| Voice signatures | Diction, sentence shape, metaphor domains, humor, directness, register shifts. |
| Sincerity and strategy | Each separately, then how they coexist or conflict. A calculating character does not imply a calculating writer. |
| Agency | Invitations, decisions, refusals, attributed partner actions, and whether choices remain available. |
| Reply affordances | Distinct consequential choices offered to the next player; repetition of one invitation is not multiple independent hooks. |
| Emotional exposure | What the viewpoint character risks revealing, and what remains protected by wit or explanation. |
| Scene movement | Starting state → action/proposal → changed possibility. A proposal is not an accepted outcome. |
| Continuity | Supported, contradicted, plausible addition, or unknown; cite the relevant canon file. |
| Systems relevance | A mechanism that changes a romantic choice, if present. Do not require a lore lecture in every post. |
| Prediction/outcome | Expected author preference and reply opportunity, then actual feedback when available. No response is not evidence of dislike. |

For later comparisons, use similar scene types and distinguish openings, replies, and conclusions. If a second reader codes a post, retain disagreements rather than manufacture agreement. Always name the sample size and version of the method (initial version: 1).

## Revision evaluation: two independent axes

**Craft:** grammar, referent clarity, physical blocking, pacing, continuity, and a playable handoff.

**Writer alignment:** preservation of supported voice features, stated purpose, desired scene focus, ambiguity the author wants to keep, and recipient preferences actually expressed or demonstrated. Unknown recipient preferences stay unknown. A smoother revision can be less recognizable; a more recognizable revision can retain deliberate roughness.

For each meaningful change, record benefit, voice cost, whether it requires new canon or another player's action, and confidence. Mechanical editing preserves content. Alignment editing may change emphasis but must label additions and preserve partner authorship. Offer optional changes rather than quietly optimizing toward the analyst's taste.

Ask for lightweight calibration when convenient: which version or line feels most yours, what should remain rough, and what response were you hoping to invite? This is optional feedback, not a prerequisite to analysis. Compare predictions with selections over time; do not invent a percentage before a useful sample exists.

## Pattern lifecycle and profiles

- **Candidate:** one independently authored post, however many examples it contains.
- **Recurring:** observed in at least three independent posts, with counterexamples sought. This is an organizational threshold, not statistical validation.
- **Contextual:** varies by scene type, character, revision stage, or partner; state the condition.
- **Retired:** contradicted, rejected by the writer, or no longer useful; retain why.

Revisions and AI-generated passages never count as independent confirmation. Record adopted edits as author preferences, not proof that the author originated the pattern. W001's self-report cannot establish W002's preferences. Keep contrary post-level evidence visible; narrow the aggregate profile instead of forcing consistency. Review profiles after each third new post, and immediately after a writer correction.

## A small systems model of the lab

**Reinforcing possibility:** attentive reading → a recognizable revision → specific author feedback → better reading. **Failure loop:** an appealing profile → edits that imitate it → those edits counted as evidence → unjustified certainty. Preserve source provenance to interrupt the failure loop.

Feedback has delays: a partner may answer much later, and immediate enthusiasm may differ from long-term satisfaction. Track intended effect, immediate response, and later interpretation separately. Treat trust, anticipation, autonomy, and mutual investment as distinct fictional states; describe possible changes with evidence rather than borrowing canon desire scores as writer measurements.
