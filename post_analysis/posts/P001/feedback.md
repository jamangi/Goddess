## What the changed ending accomplishes

The immediate choice changes from **respond to a specified activity** to **respond to a request to choose a reward**. Vincent retains initiative while leaving Eve's player room to grant that choice, ask what he has in mind, impose a condition, tease, or refuse. Acknowledging the opening does not commit Eve to the undisclosed activity.

The reader already knows Vincent's intended request through his interior narration; Eve has not heard it. The suspense is therefore partly about how he will present it and how Eve will respond, not complete informational concealment from the other player. Track reader knowledge separately from character knowledge.
