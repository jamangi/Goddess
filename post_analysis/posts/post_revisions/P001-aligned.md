## Revised prose

Vincent returned from the labyrinth and made his way to the palace. As he traversed the lavish halls towards the baths, his thoughts of violence and guerrilla strategy gave way to domestic concerns—and to what he considered his greatest personal project: the goddess herself.

He washed the repugnant crud from his skin. No amount of charisma could fully compensate for poor hygiene, particularly when negotiating with gods who could decide the fate of a million lives on a whim. He poured a bucket of water over his head, rinsing the remaining soap from his tightly coiled hair. Just as dried blood needed softening before it could be sponged away, negotiations with the goddess benefited from conversation and banter. She prized authenticity as though it were a wondrous fruit. That left him room to perform, and sometimes to exploit a hidden weakness that swung a negotiation in his favor.

He toweled off and dressed before making his way to her chambers. What would he try today? What did he *want* to do? In this emotional arena, the second question was often more useful than the first. He was fond of her feet, which she often kept bare, and the praise came easily when he meant it.

He found her sitting in a chair with one leg crossed over the other, leisurely paging through a book.

“Lady Eve. May I choose the reward for this latest victory? I wish for nothing more than to massage your lovely feet as we negotiate and express ourselves.”

He sat on the ground before her.

“Place your feet in my humble lap, goddess.”

He patted his thighs and waited for her answer.
