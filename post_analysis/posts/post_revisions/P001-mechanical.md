## Revised prose

Vincent returned from the labyrinth and made his way to the palace. He began to relax as he traversed the lavish halls towards the baths. His thoughts of violence and guerrilla strategy melted away, replaced by domestic thoughts involving what he considered to be his greatest personal project: the goddess herself.

He went to the bath and washed the repugnant crud from his skin. No amount of charisma could fully compensate for a lack of hygiene, particularly when negotiating with the gods, who could be fickle and could decide the fate of a million lives in a single day on a whim. He poured a bucket of water over his head, washing away any remaining soap residue from his curly, wiry hair. Just as crusty blood had to be softened with soapy water before it could be sponged off, successful negotiations with the goddess required softening her up with conversation and banter. This particular goddess loved authenticity as if it were a wondrous fruit, and so there was a lot of space to perform when speaking with her—enough space, in fact, to exploit a hidden weakness of hers that would sometimes swing a negotiation in his favor.

He toweled off and dressed himself before navigating to the goddess’s chambers. What would he try with her today, he pondered? What did he *want* to do? Somehow, thinking selfishly was actually far more effective in this emotional arena than thinking purely rationally. He supposed he was rather fond of her feet, which she often kept bare. He loved to adore her feet right in front of her, as the praise was always authentic and filled with his desire, and thus would usually land.

He entered her rooms and found her sitting in a chair with one leg crossed over the other, leisurely paging through a book.

“Lady Eve. May I choose the reward for this latest victory?”

“I wish for nothing more than to massage your lovely feet as we negotiate and express ourselves.”

He sat on the ground before her so that her raised leg hovered in front of his face.

“Place your feet in my humble lap, goddess.”

Patting his lap, he invited her to place her heels on his thighs.
